About

A practical, hands-on developer

I enjoy web development and technology because they let me turn ideas and problems into things people can actually use. I have been designing and building websites since 2020 for businesses, institutions, organisations and individuals.

I currently work as a Web Developer and IT Support professional at Don Dada Technologies, collaborating with a team on client websites. I also build and maintain my own projects independently.

I also work with AI and business automation, and I handle practical IT support, including hardware repairs, troubleshooting and software installation.
